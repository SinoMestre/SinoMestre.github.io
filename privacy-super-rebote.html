Privacy Policy — Super Rebote

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Who we are

The Super Rebote app (br.com.wdalm.superrebote) is developed and maintained by William Dalmagre Lourenço. For any question about this policy, write to sinomestre@gmail.com.

What the game stores on your device

Super Rebote stores on your own device:

  • how far you have got and which levels you have cleared;
  • how many stars you earned on each level;
  • your high score and your total points;
  • which bonus levels you have unlocked;
  • your sound and music settings;
  • the fact that you bought the full version, so it can be unlocked.

This data is never sent to us. If you uninstall the app, it is deleted with it.

The game asks for no sign-up and no password, and collects no email address, phone number, postal address, location, calendar, photos or contacts. The connection is used only to fetch ads — and for that reason the free version needs one in order to run. Buying the full version lets you play with no connection at all.

Advertising

For players who have not bought the full version, Super Rebote shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To decide which ads to show, AdMob may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID);
  • approximate location data (derived from the IP address);
  • information about the device and about interaction with the ads;
  • diagnostic and crash data from the advertising service itself.

This collection is carried out by Google, not by us: we receive none of this data. The rules are described in Google's privacy policy, available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Before showing any personalised advertising, the app presents a consent screen where you choose whether to accept. You can also turn off the advertising identifier in the Android settings, under Google → Ads.

Buying the full version removes the ads, and from that point the app loads nothing further from AdMob.

In-app purchase

The game offers a single optional purchase, the full version, which unlocks every world and removes the ads. The transaction is processed by Google Play: we do not receive, see or store your card details or payment account. We only store, on your own device, the fact that the purchase was made, so that what you bought can be unlocked.

If you change phones or reinstall the game, the “already bought” button restores the purchase through your Google account, at no further cost.

Children

The content of Super Rebote is suitable for any age, but the app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children. If you are responsible for a child and believe data has been collected improperly, write to sinomestre@gmail.com and we will deal with it.

Your rights

The data kept on your device is entirely under your control: to erase it, clear the app's data in the Android settings or uninstall the app. The game itself also has an ERASE MY PROGRESS button in its options.

For the data handled by Google through AdMob advertising, requests for access, correction or deletion must be made to Google, through the channels listed in its own privacy policy.

This applies wherever you live, including requests made under the GDPR in Europe, the LGPD in Brazil, or the CCPA in California.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this same address, with the date at the top updated.
