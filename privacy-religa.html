Privacy Policy — Religa

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Who we are

The Religa app (br.com.wdalm.religa) is developed and maintained by William Dalmagre Lourenço. For any question about this policy, contact us at sinomestre@gmail.com.

What the game stores on your device

Religa stores on your own device:

  • the levels you completed and the stars for each one, in the City and in the packs;
  • your coins, hints, purchased themes and the theme in use;
  • your Time Attack best scores;
  • the daily reward calendar and the star chest;
  • your sound, vibration and language preferences;
  • which game pieces have already been explained to you;
  • how many levels you have won, to know when to show ads;
  • which purchases you made, to unlock what you bought.

This data is not sent to us. If you uninstall the app, it is deleted along with it.

The game does not ask for sign-up, login or a password and does not collect your name, e-mail, phone number, address, precise location, calendar, photos or contact list.

Advertising

Religa shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To choose which ads to show, AdMob may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID);
  • approximate location data (from your IP address);
  • information about the device and about your interaction with the ads.

This collection is done by Google, not by us. The rules are described in Google's privacy policy, available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Where the law of your country requires it (for example, in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app shows a consent screen before showing personalized ads, where you choose whether to accept. You can also turn off the advertising identifier in your Android settings, under Google → Ads.

In-app purchases

The game offers optional purchases (level packs, a bundle with all of them, and hints). Any purchase also removes the full-screen ads. The transaction is processed by Google Play: we do not receive, see or store your card or payment account details. We only keep, on your own device, the information that the purchase was made.

Children

Religa's content is suitable for all ages, but the app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children. Ads are set to show only content suitable for all ages. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that data was collected improperly, write to sinomestre@gmail.com and we will handle it.

Your rights (LGPD, GDPR and similar laws)

The data kept on your device is fully under your control: to delete it, just clear the app's data in the Android settings or uninstall it.

For the data processed by Google in AdMob ads, requests for access, correction or deletion must be made to Google, through the channels listed in its privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this same address, with the update date at the top changed.
